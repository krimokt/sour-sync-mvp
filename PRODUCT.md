# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

B2B buyers and procurement managers evaluating an overseas sourcing/trading company (e.g. industrial equipment, water pumps, factory goods). They arrive from Google or a referral, often on mobile, and are making a high-stakes, high-trust purchasing decision. They need to quickly judge: "Is this company real, capable, and safe to wire money to?" Each storefront is one tenant on the SourSync multi-tenant platform; the homepage is a shared template themed per tenant (accent color, copy, imagery, metrics).

## Product Purpose

Give every SourSync tenant a credible, enterprise-grade storefront homepage that converts cautious B2B buyers into quote requests, and that ranks in Google for the tenant's brand, products, and target keywords. Success = the page reads as a legitimate, established supplier (evidence over adjectives) and drives sourcing-request submissions.

## Brand Personality

Credible, industrial, precise. Trust is earned through evidence — quantified metrics, named clients, real project photos, certifications — not through marketing adjectives. Confident but not loud; the restraint itself signals seriousness. Voice: specific nouns and verbs, plain claims a buyer can verify.

## Anti-references

- Consumer SaaS landing pages: gradient-soaked heroes, hero-metric template, playful illustrations, "supercharge / seamless / world-class" copy.
- Generic AI-template look: tiny uppercase tracked eyebrow over every section, identical icon-card grids, glassmorphism, over-rounded 32px+ cards, gradient text.
- Anything that feels like a startup pitch rather than an established industrial supplier. North star for credibility-through-evidence: rikaz.kz (clean white, dark ink, one accent, prominent quantified stats, real project portfolio, client logos, certifications).

## Design Principles

1. **Evidence over adjectives.** Lead with numbers, named clients, real photos, certifications. Every trust claim should be something a buyer could verify.
2. **Quantified credibility, early.** Surface founding year and hard metrics high on the page, not buried — a buyer should hit proof within the first screen or two.
3. **Themeable, not bespoke.** Every choice must work as a multi-tenant template driven by tenant data and a per-tenant accent color, degrading gracefully when data is absent (render only sections with real content).
4. **Industrial restraint.** Clean white base, dark ink, one accent, generous spacing. The calm, precise layout is itself a trust signal; avoid decoration that reads as a template.
5. **Built to rank.** Semantic structure, real headings, fast images, and indexable content so the credibility work doubles as SEO.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA: body text ≥ 4.5:1, large text ≥ 3:1. Dark ink on white base; muted text bumped toward ink, never light-gray-for-elegance.
- Every animation has a `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` alternative (crossfade/instant).
- Planned multi-language support (EN / Chinese / Arabic / Russian / +1) requires full RTL layout support for Arabic and correct `lang`/`dir` + `hreflang` handling.
